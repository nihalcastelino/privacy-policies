Privacy Policy

What we collect, what we do not, and what you can ask us to do about it.

PrivacyTermsDelete accountSupport
In short. MedicalOS collects your email address, an account identifier and your study progress. That is all. We do not sell data, we do not run advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

1. Who we are

MedicalOS is an anatomy learning application for medical students. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. It is written to meet the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) and applies to the mobile applications and the web application.

Grievance Officer and data contact

Email: nihal@cloudzzy.co.uk

2. What we collect

DataWhyRequired
Email addressTo create your account and sign you inRequired

Study progress is the product. It is what lets the app resume where you stopped, schedule your revision, and show your streak.

3. What we do not collect

So that there is no ambiguity, and because app stores ask us to be specific:

  • No location data, precise or approximate.
  • No advertising identifier. The app contains no advertising and no advertising SDK.
  • No push notification tokens. The app sends no push notifications.
  • No camera, microphone, photo, contact, calendar or file access.
  • No health or fitness data. What you study is education, not a record about your body.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking.
  • No financial or payment information. MedicalOS is free to use.

4. How we use it

  • To run the app — signing you in, storing your progress, scheduling your revision.
  • To personalise your study — choosing the next lesson and deciding which cards are due.
  • To keep your account working across devices — your progress syncs between the app and the web.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling unrelated to study, or automated decisions with legal effect.

5. Sign-in

You can sign in with Google, with Apple, or with a one-time link sent to your email address. There is no password to set or forget. When you use Google or Apple, they tell us your email address and, if you allow it, your name. We do not receive your password or any other information from them.

If you use Apple’s Hide My Email, Apple gives us a relay address instead of your own. That works normally, but it is a different address from your Google or email sign-in, so it creates a separate account with separate progress.

6. Who processes your data

We use service providers who process data only on our instructions and only to run the service:

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting.
  • Anthropic — powers optional AI study features on the web application. Your question is sent to be answered and the response returned. We do not store AI conversation transcripts.
  • Netlify and Railway — application and interface hosting.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

7. How long we keep it

Your account data and study progress are kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, personal data is removed as described on the account deletion page. Encrypted backups may retain remnants until their normal rotation expires; they are isolated from ordinary use and are not used to restore a deleted account.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDPA you may ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it, and you may withdraw consent. Deletion is available in the app at any time and needs no request — see the deletion page. For anything else, email nihal@cloudzzy.co.uk. We respond within 30 days.

9. Children

MedicalOS is built for medical students and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to the database is restricted by row-level security so that your progress is readable only by your own account. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell affected users and the Data Protection Board without undue delay if a breach puts personal data at risk.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for material changes, tell you in the app. Continuing to use MedicalOS after a change means the updated policy applies.
