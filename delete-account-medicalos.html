Account and Data Deletion

Delete your account from inside the app, and exactly what happens when you do.

PrivacyTermsDelete accountSupport
You can delete your account yourself, from inside the app, at any time. No request, no waiting for us.

1. Delete from the app

On your phone:

  1. Open MedicalOS and sign in.
  2. Go to More.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Read what will be removed, then type DELETE to confirm.

On the web, and in MedicalOS for Windows: open Profile and use the Delete account card. The confirmation is the same.

2. Delete by email

If you cannot sign in, email nihal@cloudzzy.co.uk from the address on your account, with the subject Account Deletion Request.

Please do not send

Passwords, sign-in links, patient information or identity documents. We will never ask for them.

We acknowledge within 7 days and complete verified deletion within 30 days. The data controller is CLOUDZZY LTD, 320 Firecrest Court, Centre Park, Warrington, WA1 1RG, United Kingdom.

4. What is kept, and why

Deleting your account always deletes your sign-in. It never leaves a disabled copy of your account behind. A few records are kept because other people’s learning or our security depends on them, and they are kept without your name:

  • AI usage records (provider, model, cost and a fingerprint, never your text) stay with the link to your account removed.
  • Records of who edited or approved teaching content stay with your name removed. This matters only if you were a lecturer or contributor.
  • The access-audit log, which records class and institution actions for security, keeps its entries (what happened and when) but loses your account identifier when you delete your account.
  • If you are a lecturer, the classes you own are removed, the messages you sent are withdrawn from students’ inboxes, and the topics you wrote are withheld from students while rights over them are resolved. A restricted record of ownership and permissions is kept for up to 90 days, then deleted together with the withheld content unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • Backups, server logs and our sign-in provider’s event log are covered by section 9 of the Privacy Policy. In short: backups rotate out within 30 days; server request logs are kept for 7 days; the entries in the sign-in provider’s event log that name your account or email address are deleted when you delete your account, and its platform logs expire within 28 days.
  • Short-lived counters in our rate-limit store (Upstash) hold only your account identifier or IP address and expire by themselves, the longest within 40 days.

Either way, every piece of personal data listed in section 3 is removed, and you cannot sign in again afterwards.

5. Before you delete

  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Your streak, review schedule and everything you have studied will be gone.
  • If you joined classes, you leave them. If you are a lecturer, your classes end for your students.
  • Deleting your MedicalOS account does not delete your Google or Apple account.
  • If you used MedicalOS for Windows, deleting the account does not remove the three entries the app keeps in Windows Credential Manager (named org.medicalos.desktop.store for the Microsoft Store version and org.medicalos.desktop for the installer version). They hold a random device identifier and a key, not your account, and you can delete them in Windows Credential Manager.

6. Other requests

You can ask for a copy of your data or have it corrected without deleting your account. See the Privacy Policy or email nihal@cloudzzy.co.uk.
