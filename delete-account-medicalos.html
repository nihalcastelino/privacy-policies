Account and Data Deletion

Delete your account from inside the app, and exactly what happens when you do.

PrivacyTermsDelete accountSupport
You can delete your account yourself, from inside the app, at any time. No request, no waiting for us.

1. Delete from the app

On your phone:

  1. Open MedicalOS and sign in.
  2. Go to More.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Read what will be removed, then type DELETE to confirm.

On the web: open Profile and use the Delete account card. The confirmation is the same.

2. Delete by email

If you cannot sign in, email nihal@cloudzzy.co.uk from the address on your account, with the subject Account Deletion Request.

Please do not send

Passwords, sign-in links, patient information or identity documents. We will never ask for them.

We acknowledge within 7 days and complete verified deletion within 30 days.

3. What is removed

Your activity records and session historyDeleted
Your email address and sign-in accountDeleted or anonymised — see below

4. Deleted, or anonymised — and why the difference

For most people the account is deleted outright: the sign-in record goes, and nothing identifying remains.

If you are a faculty member who has authored or reviewed clinical teaching content, some records cannot be removed without destroying the review history that other people’s learning depends on — who approved a lesson, and when. In that case your personal data is still fully removed, your name is detached from the content you authored, and the sign-in record is retained only as an anonymised, disabled stub that can no longer be used to sign in and no longer identifies you.

Either way, every piece of personal data listed in section 3 is removed, and you cannot sign in again afterwards.

5. Before you delete

  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Your streak, review schedule and everything you have studied will be gone.
  • Deleting your MedicalOS account does not delete your Google or Apple account.

6. Other requests

You can ask for a copy of your data or have it corrected without deleting your account. See the Privacy Policy or email nihal@cloudzzy.co.uk.
