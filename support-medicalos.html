Get help

Email nihal@cloudzzy.co.uk. We read every message and aim to reply within 3 working days. MedicalOS is provided by CLOUDZZY LTD, 320 Firecrest Court, Centre Park, Warrington, WA1 1RG, United Kingdom.

What to include

The email address on your account, the device you are using, and what you expected to happen. Screenshots help. Please do not send passwords or sign-in links.

Common questions

I did not receive my sign-in link. Check your spam folder first. Links expire after a short time, so request a fresh one rather than reusing an old email. If it still does not arrive, sign in with Google or Apple instead.

My progress is missing on another device. Progress syncs when you are signed in to the same account. If you signed in with Google on one device and Apple on another, those are separate accounts with separate progress — sign in with the method you first used.

MedicalOS says I am signed in on too many devices. A student account can be signed in on only a few devices at a time. Open MedicalOS on a device that is still signed in, go to your devices list, and sign out one you no longer use. If you have lost a device or used up your sign-outs, email us and we will clear your devices.

I want to remove everything MedicalOS left on my Windows PC. Sign out of the app, uninstall it (with the installer version, tick “Delete the application data” in the uninstaller), then delete the entries named org.medicalos.desktop.store (Microsoft Store version) or org.medicalos.desktop (installer version) in Windows Credential Manager.

I want to delete my account. You can do it yourself in the app. See the deletion page.

I found a mistake in the anatomy. Please tell us. Include the lesson name and what you believe is wrong.

Reporting a security issue

Email nihal@cloudzzy.co.uk with the subject Security. Please give us a reasonable chance to fix an issue before disclosing it publicly.
